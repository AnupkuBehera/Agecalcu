Key Developmental Milestones by Completed Month

Standard pediatric milestones commonly monitored during the first 24 completed calendar months:

2 Months
Social Smile & Visual Tracking

Calms when spoken to or picked up; tracks moving objects with eyes across midline.

6 Months
Sitting & Solid Food Introduction

Rolls from tummy to back; sits without support; starts babbling with consonants (da-da, ba-ba).

12 Months
First Steps & Words

Pulls to stand; waves bye-bye; calls parents mama/dada; pincer grasp fully developed.

18 Months
Language Explosion & Running

Speaks 10+ words; walks independently; drinks from an open cup; points to show interest.

24 Months
Two-Word Phrases & Running

Combines two words (e.g. 'more milk'); kicks a ball; runs with steady balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate age in months and days without dividing by 30? +

Our engine tracks actual calendar months. For instance, moving from January 15 to February 15 counts as exactly 1 month, regardless of whether January has 31 days. Remaining days are calculated from the previous completed month boundary.

When does a baby turn 1 month old? +

A baby born on March 14th reaches exactly 1 month old on April 14th. If born on January 31st, they reach 1 month old on February 28th (or Feb 29th in a leap year).
