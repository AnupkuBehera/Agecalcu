Frequently Asked Questions

Will 2040 be a leap year? +

Yes. 2040 is divisible by 4 and not a century year, meaning it has 366 days and a February 29th.

How do you calculate retirement age in 2040? +

Standard retirement ages in most pension and social security programs range from 60 to 67. If your age in 2040 is between 60 and 67, you will be in your active retirement transition window.
