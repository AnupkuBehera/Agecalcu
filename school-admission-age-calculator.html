Frequently Asked Questions

Can a 5-year-old child take admission in Class 1? +

Under NEP 2020 directives, children under 6 years as on 31st March are not permitted to enroll directly in Class 1 in CBSE, ICSE, and Kendriya Vidyalayas. They should complete Balvatika 3 (UKG) first.

What documents are required to prove child age for school admission? +

The official Birth Certificate issued by the Municipal Corporation / Registrar of Births and Deaths is universally mandated across all schools in India.
