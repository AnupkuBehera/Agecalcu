Frequently Asked Questions

What if my child misses a scheduled vaccine date?

Most vaccines do not need to be restarted if a dose is delayed; you simply continue from the next dose according to your pediatrician's catch-up immunization guidance.

Why are there both government UIP and IAP schedules?

The Government Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) covers essential vaccines for all citizens. The Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) includes additional protective vaccines such as Pneumococcal (PCV), Rotavirus, Varicella (Chickenpox), Hepatitis A, and HPV.

Can I export these immunization dates to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar?

Yes! Click the 'Download .ICS Calendar' button to generate a standard iCalendar file containing all upcoming doses, which automatically imports into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.
