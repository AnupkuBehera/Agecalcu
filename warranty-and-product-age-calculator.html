Frequently Asked Questions

How is product age and warranty expiration calculated? ↓

Product age is calculated using calendar subtraction from your original purchase date to today. Warranty expiration adds the manufacturer warranty period (e.g. 1 year, 2 years) to the purchase date to evaluate remaining days.

Can I use this for vehicle and car age calculation? ↓

Yes! Enter your vehicle registration or delivery date to track vehicle age in years, months, and days for insurance renewal, roadworthiness inspection, and warranty status.

Are my receipt details or purchase dates uploaded anywhere? ↓

No. All calculations run strictly client-side inside your browser. No product or purchase information is sent to any server.
