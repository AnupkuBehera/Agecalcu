3. Educational & Mathematical Disclaimer

While Dobcal utilizes exact mathematical calendar subtraction (day-by-day borrowing) rather than imprecise 365.25 approximations, all calculations are provided for informational and planning purposes only:

  • Government Recruitment & Exam Forms: Always verify your final calculated age against the official notification gazette (e.g. UPSC, SSC, state PSCs) for specific age-reckoning rules.
  • Medical & Pediatric Estimates: Calculations for gestational age, pregnancy due dates (Naegele's rule), and vaccination schedules are based on standard clinical guidelines (IAP/WHO) and should never replace formal medical consultation with a licensed physician.
  • Superannuation & Pensions: Pension calculations follow Central Civil Services (CCS) standards, but specific service deductions, leaves, and qualifying periods must be verified through official pension disbursing authorities.
