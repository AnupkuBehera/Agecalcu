Frequently Asked Questions

At what age is a dog or cat considered a senior pet? +

Giant dogs become seniors as early as 5-6 years old. Medium dogs reach senior status around 7-8 years. Small dogs and cats are generally considered seniors at 10-11 years of age.

What is the UC San Diego epigenetic clock for dogs? +

In 2020, researchers at UC San Diego published a DNA methylation formula: Human Age = 16 * ln(Dog Age) + 31. This showed that young dogs age very rapidly at the cellular level compared to humans.
