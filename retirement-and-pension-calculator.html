Understanding Government Superannuation Rules

Under Fundamental Rule 56 and the Central Civil Services (CCS) Pension Rules in India, government servants retire from service on the afternoon of the last day of the month in which they attain the retirement age (typically 60 years).

The 1st of the Month Exception: An employee whose date of birth falls on the 1st day of a month officially retires on the afternoon of the last day of the immediately preceding month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is retirement date calculated for government employees? ↓

Under Central Civil Services (CCS) rules, government employees retire on the afternoon of the last day of the month in which they attain the age of 60. However, if the birth date falls on the 1st of the month, retirement occurs on the afternoon of the last day of the preceding month.

What is qualifying service for pension eligibility? ↓

Qualifying service is the total period of continuous government service rendered after attaining the age of 18. A minimum of 10 years of qualifying service is required to receive regular monthly pension, and 20 years for maximum full pension under CCS Pension Rules.

How is working days remaining until retirement calculated? ↓

Working days remaining counts all future weekdays (Monday through Friday) between today and your superannuation date, excluding standard weekends.
