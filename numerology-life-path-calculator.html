Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Life Path Number calculated? ↓

Your Life Path Number is calculated by reducing each component of your birth date (day, month, and year) to single digits by summing their individual numbers, unless a Master Number (11, 22, or 33) is reached, then summing those three values.

What are Master Numbers in numerology? ↓

Master Numbers are the double-digit numbers 11, 22, and 33. In Pythagorean numerology, they are not reduced to single digits (2, 4, or 6) because they represent amplified spiritual insight, master building capability, and humanitarian teaching.

What is the difference between Life Path Number and Birth Day Number? ↓

The Birth Day Number (Mulank) is derived solely from the day of the month you were born (reduced to 1-9) and represents innate natural talents, whereas the Life Path Number encompasses your entire date of birth and represents your life's overarching purpose.
