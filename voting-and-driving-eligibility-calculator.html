Election Commission of India (ECI) Voter Enrollment Rules

Under the amended Representation of the People Act, 17-year-olds can apply in advance for voter registration across 4 annual qualifying dates:

1st January Cutoff

If turning 18 between Oct 2 & Jan 1, eligible for enrollment in Q1 revisions.

1st April Cutoff

If turning 18 between Jan 2 & Apr 1, eligible for enrollment in Q2 revisions.

1st July Cutoff

If turning 18 between Apr 2 & Jul 1, eligible for enrollment in Q3 revisions.

1st October Cutoff

If turning 18 between Jul 2 & Oct 1, eligible for enrollment in Q4 revisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for a Voter ID card before turning 18? +

Yes. Youth above 17 years can apply in advance using Form 6 on the NVSP / Voter Helpline App. Your application is automatically processed when you turn 18 on the nearest qualifying date.

What documents prove age for a driving license in India? +

Acceptable proof of age documents include 10th standard mark sheet, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Birth Certificate.
