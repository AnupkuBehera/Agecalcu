Frequently Asked Questions

Is my family's birthday data private and secure? ↓

Yes, 100%. All family member names, relationships, and birth dates are stored exclusively inside your browser's local storage (localStorage). No data is ever transmitted to any external server.

How does the birthday countdown work? ↓

The calculator compares today's date with each person's next upcoming birthday in the calendar year (or the following year if already passed) and sorts all entries chronologically by days remaining.

Can I export family birthdays to my calendar or another device? ↓

Yes! You can export your saved entries directly as a standard .ics calendar file or backup JSON, which can be imported into Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or another browser.
