Frequently Asked Questions

Which date certificate is considered proof of Date of Birth for Govt Jobs? +

In almost all Indian government recruitment (UPSC, SSC, State PSCs), the date of birth recorded in your Class 10th (Matriculation / Secondary School) Examination Certificate is treated as conclusive proof.

Does EWS category get age relaxation in UPSC or SSC? +

No. Economically Weaker Section (EWS) candidates receive vertical reservation in vacancies, but no age relaxation or extra number of attempts is granted under current Central Government recruitment rules.

What happens if my birthday falls exactly on the cutoff date (e.g. 1st August)? +

By official DoPT interpretation, an individual born on 1st August turns an exact integer year older at 00:00 hours on 1st August. If the upper age limit is 32, a person born on 1st August 1994 completes 32 years on 1st August 2026 and remains eligible.
