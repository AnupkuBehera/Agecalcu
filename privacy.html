Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Privacy Policy & Data Security

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Worldwide

1. 100% Client-Side Execution (Zero Server Storage)

Dobcal operates on a strict **zero-knowledge, client-side privacy model**. When you enter your date of birth, exam cutoff dates, pregnancy last menstrual period (LMP), child vaccination milestones, or retirement service records, all calculations occur strictly inside your device's browser memory using vanilla JavaScript.

**At no point are your dates, names, or inputs transmitted across the network, stored on a remote server, or recorded in any database.**

2. Zero Tracking Cookies & Zero Advertising Networks

Dobcal does not use advertising identifiers, marketing trackers, retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads Remarketing), or third-party behavioral trackers.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal data of any kind.

3. Local Storage Preferences

Dobcal utilizes browser `localStorage` strictly for:

  • Theme Preference: Storing whether you prefer dark mode or light mode (`theme: 'dark'`).
  • Language Selection: Remembering your chosen language code across sessions.
  • Family Birthday Manager: Storing your personal list of saved family birthdays. This list remains isolated on your device and can be cleared at any time with 1 tap.

4. Offline Progressive Web App (PWA)

Dobcal includes a service worker (`sw.js`) that caches static stylesheets, scripts, and vector assets. This allows the application to execute entirely without an internet connection, ensuring continuous privacy even in offline environments.

5. Global Regulatory Compliance

Because Dobcal does not collect, transmit, or process personally identifiable information (PII), it inherently complies with:

  • European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)
  • India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)
  • Brazil Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD)
