About Dobcal Chronometer

The story of building an uncompromising precision instrument for chronological time.

1. Why Dobcal Was Created

Most date calculators on the internet suffer from two fatal flaws:

  1. Disrespect for User Attention: Cluttered with intrusive video popups, banner ads, tracking scripts, and cookie consent walls that slow down page loads and drain mobile batteries.
  2. Flawed Mathematics: Relying on naive approximations (such as dividing total elapsed days by 365.25), resulting in calculation errors of 1 to 3 days that cause candidate disqualification on government exam forms and school admissions.

Dobcal was built to reverse this: an ultra-premium, privacy-first instrument designed with the craftsmanship of a Swiss chronometer and the speed of modern web engineering.

2. Mathematical & Scientific Foundations

Dobcal strictly implements verified domain standards across its 26 tools:

Calendar Borrowing Algorithm

Exact Gregorian subtraction with real calendar days (28–31) borrowed from preceding months, ensuring full legal compliance.

Central Civil Services (CCS) Rules

Implements Rule 56(a) for superannuation: retirement on the afternoon of the last day of the month, with 1st-of-month exception.

Naegele's Obstetric Rule

Calculates estimated date of delivery (EDD) and gestational development according to clinical 280-day biological cycles.

Keplerian Orbital Physics

Calculates planetary age based on NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory orbital sidereal periods from Mercury to Neptune.
