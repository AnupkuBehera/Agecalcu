Frequently Asked Questions

Who is considered older when two people have different birth dates? +

The person with the earlier chronological birth date was born first and is the older individual.

What does the 'Half Your Age Plus Seven' rule indicate? +

It is a widespread social heuristic for romantic age gaps: taking the older person's age, dividing by 2, and adding 7 gives the minimum conventional dating age. For example, for a 30-year-old, the minimum conventional age is (30/2) + 7 = 22.
